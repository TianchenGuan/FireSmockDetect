## 1. Research question and pre-registered design

- Question (reports/v4/PREREGISTRATION_V4.md; STATUS.md "ITERATION 4" header): does generic/non-target smoke data (G) transfer to the deployed task, alone, mixed, or as a curriculum, better than simply adding target-domain data (T) to the existing corpus (E); does adapting the DINOv3 backbone beat YOLO at matched false-positive rate; does extended sequence data improve early warning.
- Arms, exposure-matched at ~2.31M image exposures each (STATUS.md 08:38Z, 11:40Z): A_ctrl_E = E only (38,454 images, 60 ep); B_Gonly = G only (14,901 images, 158 ep; selection on a strict source-only gval of 310 images); E_ET = E+T (51,420 lines, 45 ep); C_EGT = E + 2xG + 2xT (94,188 effective lines, 25 ep); D = curriculum D_pre (G) -> D_adapt (E+T + 15% G replay, 26 ep) (reports/v4/DATA_CARD_V4.md §3).
- Budget ceilings: 16 nodes / 128 L40S concurrent, 384 node-h / 3,072 GPU-h in a 24 h window (cluster/v4/BUDGET_V4.json); all submissions reserved through `cluster/v4/registry.py`.
- Pre-registration (frozen ~19:00Z, before any test prediction; PREREGISTRATION_V4.md): systems S0-S4 fixed by validation rules (3-seed means; median-seed rule; S2 = higher 3-seed boreal R@1% with a <1pt tie going to E_ET; S4 = best sdis-val R@5% among the DINO runs); two threshold regimes (SOURCE-ONLY: sdis-val conf at 5/10/20% neg-FP; TARGET-ADAPTED: boreal-val conf at 1/5%); the deployed default changes only if a system ties expE within 1pt on sdis TEST R@10% AND beats it by >=10pt on boreal TEST R@1%FP AND loses no FIgLib events AND passes latency/integration.
- Deviations, stated: (i) D_curr was evaluated on test as a **post-hoc addition** — it was not a pre-registered candidate; its 3-seed median became available only after the test job launched and the shipped checkpoint (`v4dada_s1`) coincidentally equals the median-seed pick (STATUS.md 02:40Z, 03:00Z); (ii) the DINO half of the test was launched at 02:31Z by the pre-restart coordinator session with exactly the pre-registered systems and thresholds and was **not re-run** (a re-run would break "evaluate once"; STATUS.md 03:00Z; reports/v4/test/.DINO_EVALUATED_ONCE).
- Test package sealed 2026-09-14T18:38:37Z, tar sha256 f7cc4ad7…, 0 path / 0 sha256 overlap against 84,680 train/val images (reports/v4/LOCKBOX_V4_FROZEN.md).

## 11. Limitations and honest non-results

- The research hypothesis failed: curriculum/generic pretraining does not beat straightforward E+T on val, and on the fixed-camera TEST every T-arm loses recall to the existing recipe (§6.1). D_curr's advantage is 3 seeds on val but post-hoc on test.
- The val aerial gain (+31pt) did not reproduce on the test burn (+0.3-1.7pt): a one-burn val set produced a misleading effect size (§6.2).
- Denominators are tiny and dependent: boreal val/test = 1 burn each (217 / 2 negatives); forest val ~3 events, test 4; boreal 1% FP = 2 images. sdis TEST 1% FP = ~9 of 917 negatives.
- No negatives from any new domain (329 v4 negatives vs 27,538 positives) -> forest matched-FP undefined, no per-domain calibration, and the temporal extension became a domain shortcut (DATA_CARD_V4.md §7; EARLY_WARNING_V4.md §4). The target-adapted YOLO thresholds (0.048-0.30) that unlock forest recall are unusable on the fixed-camera service (T25 (c)).
- No new onset supervision (0/26); "earlier detection" was not demonstrated; T-arms' +2 FIgLib events come with ~2x pre-visible alerts (§5).
- DINO results are confounded (B5 batch size / steps / end-only checkpoint; best.pt selected on a sdis-val subset). Adapted-model thresholds do not transfer across domains (FIgLib) and their strict-FP test numbers are worse than YOLO.
- The firesense negative pool is both a training negative for B/C/D_adapt and the replay false-alert pool -> optimistic for those arms. Licensing: AuSmoke and the MultiNatSmoke aggregate are unlicensed; DFS unobtainable.
- FIgLib-39 is historical/open (used since iteration 1), not a lockbox. The compute ledger has no utilization telemetry.
- Test protocol deviations (§1): D_curr post-hoc; DINO half launched by a concurrent coordinator session; both stated, neither re-run.

## 12. Recommendations for v5

1. **Negatives from the new domains first**: smoke-free aerial/drone flights over forest and Australian bush fixed-camera footage matched in camera/season/viewpoint — unblocks matched-FP on forest, per-domain thresholds, and removes the shortcut (DATA_CARD_V4.md §7; EARLY_WARNING_V4.md verdict 6).
2. **Real onset sequences**: untrimmed recordings starting before first visible smoke with a human-marked onset frame (boreal Subset-B positive clips are candidates).
3. **Per-domain threshold calibration** as a first-class deliverable; never transfer a sdis threshold or a YOLO threshold to a DINO model (§4, §8, T27).
4. **Curriculum arm**: pre-register D_curr as a candidate (it is now 3 seeds on val) and test it properly; explore G-as-initialization further since G-as-mixture is dead.
5. **DFS / AuSmoke licensing**: human Baidu download of DFS.rar + fireLabel.rar and legal review; confirm AuSmoke/FESB terms with the authors before use beyond the internal prototype (DATA_CARD_V4.md §7).
6. More independent burns/flights per domain (other UAV operators, other seasons) so val/test hold >1 event; report per flight/event, never per frame. If DINO is pursued, LoRA-L is the efficiency point (T12/T13) and its strict-FP behaviour needs more negatives before any claim.

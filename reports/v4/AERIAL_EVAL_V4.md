## 1. Question and scope

The deployed product is a fixed-camera smoke detector (pyro-sdis). The iteration-4 aerial question is whether the same detector, with or without target-domain data, detects smoke in genuine drone RGB imagery — the "drone uploads image -> server returns boxes + alert" path — and whether that can be *demonstrated* rather than assumed. Two aerial sources exist (reports/v4/DATA_CARD_V4.md §1.1):

| AuSmoke (ANU, ACT Australia) DJI units | DJI (model unrecorded), ~1 fps frames | val: 1 flight (g_dji_0792); test: 1 flight `g_dji_20230922` (3 videos, 258 frames, one a staged TEST_RUN) | positives-only | 0 |

Aerial labels are a single "large box" around the plume (4,670 of 4,954 boreal images have exactly one box; boreal_CARD.md). ## 6. What is and is not demonstrated

Demonstrated:
1. A held-out Finnish UAV burn is detected at ~0.9 frame recall by every evaluated system, including the deployed expB and the iteration-2 expE, without any aerial training data (T26).
2. Target aerial data (T) raises recall on aerial imagery — by a lot on the heinola val burn (+31pt at 1% FP), by 0.3-1.7pt on the ruokolahti test burn at comparable thresholds, and by ~45pt on the Australian flight at target-adapted thresholds (T27 (b)).
3. Thresholds do not transfer from the fixed-camera domain to aerial/forest imagery; per-domain calibration is the adaptation step (T27 (a) vs (b); reports/v4/FINAL_REPORT_V4.md §3 verdict 5).

Not demonstrated:
1. **Any false-alarm rate on aerial imagery.** 2 test negatives, 217 val negatives from one burn, 14 non-smoke clips. The deployed operating point produces 74 false episodes/h on heinola non-smoke footage (T20).
2. **Generalization across burns beyond n=1.** Four burns exist (train 2 / val 1 / test 1), one country, one season, one drone model; the val-to-test gap already moved from +31pt to ~+1pt between two burns of the same campaign. `aerial_split_v4.json` carries a `tiny_n_warning` for exactly this reason.
3. **Faint/early aerial smoke.** Boreal boxes are "large box" plumes (>5% of the frame); nothing here measures small or incipient plumes from the air, and no aerial onset sequences exist (reports/v4/DATA_CARD_V4.md §5).
4. **An Australian aerial baseline with negatives**: one flight, positives-only, one of three videos staged.

Verdict: aerial generalization is **partially demonstrated** — recall on one held-out UAV burn ≈0.9 for every system including the baselines; the false-alarm side is unverified; new-region gains are real but require target-domain threshold calibration (STATUS.md 03:55Z decisions).

## 7. Recommendations (aerial)

## Semantics / intended V5 use
- ARTIFICIAL smoke (smoke bomb), real forest background, several viewpoints/distances. No onset timestamps provided.
- Purpose tag at ingest: **onset-tool testing only** (exercise the onset-interval labelling tool and temporal windows on a
  known-artificial source). NEVER a metric set for recall/FP/early-warning; never training without a separate decision.
- Not assigned to any scenario split; no model has seen it.

## Limitations
- Smoke bomb ≠ wildfire smoke (colour, density, rise rate). Single site/campaign → 1 independent unit.
- Camera type/altitude unknown (record gives none). Ground/handheld viewpoints likely; verify by eye.
